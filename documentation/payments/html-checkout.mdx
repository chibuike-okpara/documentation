---
title: "HTML Checkout"
description: "Accept payments with a plain HTML form. No backend, no JavaScript."
---

Put a form on your page. When the customer clicks the button, they go to Modem Pay's checkout, pay, and come back to your site.

```html
<form action="https://test.checkout.modempay.com/api/pay" method="POST">
  <input type="hidden" name="public_key" value="pk_test_...">
  <input type="hidden" name="amount" value="150">
  <input type="hidden" name="currency" value="GMD">
  <input type="hidden" name="return_url" value="https://yoursite.com/thanks">
  <input type="hidden" name="cancel_url" value="https://yoursite.com/cart">
  <button type="submit">Pay GMD 150</button>
</form>
```

## Form fields

| Field | Required | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `public_key` | Yes | Your **public** key (`pk_test_…` or `pk_live_…`). Never your secret key. |
| `amount` | Yes | Amount in GMD, at least 10. |
| `currency` | No | `GMD`. |
| `return_url` | Recommended | Where the customer goes after paying. |
| `cancel_url` | Recommended | Where the customer goes if they cancel. |
| `customer_name` | No | Fills in checkout for the customer. |
| `customer_email` | No | Fills in checkout for the customer. |
| `customer_phone` | No | Fills in checkout for the customer. |
| `metadata` | No | Your own data, like an order ID. It comes back on the transaction and webhook. |

## Confirm the payment

Anyone can edit a form in their browser, including the amount. So **before you deliver anything, check the payment on your server**:

- receive the `charge.succeeded` [webhook](/documentation/core/webhooks) and check its `amount`, or
- look the payment up with your secret key.

<Tip>
  Want customers to pay without leaving your page? Use the [pay button](/documentation/payments/drop-in) or the [checkout pop-up](/documentation/payments/modem-inline) instead. Both are just as easy.
</Tip>
