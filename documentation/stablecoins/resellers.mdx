If you run your own app or exchange desk, you can offer conversions to your
users under your own brand and add a markup. Your user pays the conversion plus
your markup; Modem Pay handles the stablecoin, the conversion and the payout,
and credits your markup to your balance.

<Note>
  This page adds one field, `markup_config`, to a normal conversion. Everything
  in [Convert stablecoins to dalasi](/documentation/stablecoins/convert-to-dalasi)
  still applies.
</Note>

## How the price is built

```
pay_amount = conversion (gmd_amount at the rate) + Modem Pay fee + your markup
```

- The **conversion** and **fee** work exactly like a normal conversion.
- Your **markup** is added on top. Your user pays it, and you keep it.

## markup_config

<ParamField body="markup_config" type="object">
  <Expandable title="markup_config">
    <ParamField body="type" type="string" required>
      `fixed` for a flat USD amount, or `percentage` for a percent of the
      converted amount.
    </ParamField>
    <ParamField body="value" type="number" required>
      For `fixed`, USD (`4` means $4). For `percentage`, a percent (`2` means
      2%).
    </ParamField>
  </Expandable>
</ParamField>

<Warning>
  Your markup can be at most **10% of the converted amount**. A request above
  that is rejected with the maximum allowed, so check small amounts carefully
  when using a `fixed` markup.
</Warning>

## Example

A 2% markup on a 5,000 GMD conversion paid to the user's Wave wallet:

<CodeGroup>

```bash cURL
curl -X POST https://api.modempay.com/v1/minting/offramp \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_API_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "gmd_amount": 5000,
    "destination": "mobile_money",
    "payout_method": {
      "network": "wave",
      "account_number": "7000000",
      "account_name": "Awa Jallow"
    },
    "markup_config": { "type": "percentage", "value": 2 }
  }'
```

```typescript nodejs
const conversion = await modempay.offramp.create({
  gmd_amount: 5000,
  destination: "mobile_money",
  payout_method: {
    network: "wave",
    account_number: "7000000",
    account_name: "Awa Jallow",
  },
  markup_config: { type: "percentage", value: 2 },
});
```

</CodeGroup>

You can also size it by what the user sends, with `pay_amount` instead of
`gmd_amount`. The markup comes out of that amount, and `gmd_amount` in the
response shows what the user receives.

### Response (markup fields)

```json
{
  "gmd_amount": 5000,
  "fee": 2.55,
  "pay_amount": 74.05,
  "pay_currency": "USDT",
  "markup_share_usd": 1.4,
  "markup_config": { "type": "percentage", "value": 2 },
  "status": "pending"
}
```

`pay_amount` includes your markup, and `markup_share_usd` is what you earn, in
USD.

<Tip>
  Check the price your user will see with a
  [full quote](/documentation/stablecoins/price-estimates#full-quote) before
  creating the conversion.
</Tip>

## How you get paid

- Your user's dalasi (`gmd_amount`) goes to their wallet or your balance, as
  set by `destination`.
- Your markup is credited to **your Modem Pay balance**, as a separate entry,
  even when the user's dalasi goes to a mobile money wallet.
- With partial payments, the user's dalasi and your markup are credited in the
  same proportion as the money arrives, and complete together.

## Good to know

- The markup is locked when the conversion is created and doesn't change with
  the rate.
- The Modem Pay fee applies to the conversion as usual; your markup is yours.
