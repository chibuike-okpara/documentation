**Split Payments** share each payment between you and your partners automatically. No manual transfers, no spreadsheets.

Useful for marketplaces, schools, agencies, or any business that shares revenue.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set up your partners">
    Create a [sub-account](/documentation/split-payments/sub-accounts) for each
    partner. To pay several on one payment, put them in a
    [group](/documentation/split-payments/sub-account-groups).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attach to a payment">
    Pass `sub_account` or `sub_account_group` when you [create a payment
    intent](/documentation/split-payments/initialize-payment).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get paid">
    When the customer pays, each partner's share goes to their balance and the
    rest goes to yours.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Example

A customer pays **1,000 GMD** and the customer covers the fee, so you receive the full 1,000 GMD.

|          | One sub-account (10%) | Group (30% + 40%) |
| -------- | --------------------- | ----------------- |
| Partners | 100 GMD               | 300 GMD + 400 GMD |
| You      | 900 GMD               | 300 GMD           |

If your business pays the fee instead, the percentages apply to what's left after the fee. See [how shares are calculated](/documentation/split-payments/sub-accounts#how-the-share-is-calculated).
