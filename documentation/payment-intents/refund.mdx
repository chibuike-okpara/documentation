---
title: "Refunds"
description: "Give a customer their money back, in full or in part."
---

You can refund a completed payment within **48 hours**. The money comes from your [payout balance](/documentation/core/balances) and goes back to the customer.

## Rules

- The transaction must be `completed`, or `partially_refunded` if you've already refunded part of it.
- The original payment must be no more than **48 hours** old.
- You can refund part of a payment, and refund again later, until the total refunded reaches the original amount.
- A processing charge may apply.

## Refund a payment

Identify the payment by its transaction `id`, `reference` or `transaction_reference`. Leave out `amount` for a full refund.

<CodeGroup>
```javascript nodejs
// Full refund
await modempay.transactions.reverse("VOAO1CFEN7");

// Partial refund of 450 GMD
await modempay.transactions.reverse("VOAO1CFEN7", 450);
```

```bash cURL
curl -X POST https://api.modempay.com/v1/transactions/refund \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer sk_test_..." \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{ "reference": "VOAO1CFEN7", "amount": 450 }'
```
</CodeGroup>

A restricted key needs the `refunds:write` permission. Because refunds move money, give it only to systems that really need it.

## After a refund

- The transaction's status becomes `refunded`, or `partially_refunded` for a partial refund.
- You get a `charge.refunded` [webhook](/documentation/core/webhooks).
- The customer usually sees the money quickly, but their provider decides the exact timing. Let them know a refund is on its way.

<Tip>
  Need to give money back after 48 hours, or to a different number? Send a [payout](/documentation/payouts/mobile-money) instead.
</Tip>
