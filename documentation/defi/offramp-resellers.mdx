---
title: "Off-Ramp for Resellers"
---

If you run your own platform, you can offer off-ramp to your users and add your
own markup on top. Your users pay the off-ramp amount plus your markup; you earn
the markup as settled dalasi. Modem Pay handles the stablecoin, the conversion,
and the payout, you never touch the crypto or the fiat.

<Note>
  This page builds on the regular [Off-Ramp](/documentation/defi/offramp) flow.
  Everything there applies; this page covers only the extra `markup_config`
  field.
</Note>

## How the markup works

When you add a `markup_config`, the amount your user pays becomes
**base + markup**:

- **Base** — the stablecoin needed to settle the `gmd_amount` (plus the standard
  fee), exactly as in a regular off-ramp.
- **Markup** — your spread, added on top. Your user pays it; you earn it.

The markup is fixed when the off-ramp is created and stored as
**`markup_share_usd`** (in USD). As payment arrives, the markup is settled to
your balance proportionally alongside the base, and is fully settled when the
payment completes.

## markup_config

<ParamField body="markup_config" type="object">
  Your spread. Added to what the user pays; settled to you.

  <Expandable title="markup_config">
    <ParamField body="type" type="string" required>
      Either `fixed` (a flat USD amount) or `percentage` (a percent of the base).
    </ParamField>
    <ParamField body="value" type="number" required>
      The markup. For `fixed`, a USD amount (e.g. `4` = $4). For `percentage`,
      a percent of the base (e.g. `5` = 5%).
    </ParamField>
  </Expandable>
</ParamField>

<Warning>
  Markup is capped relative to the base amount. A request whose markup exceeds
  the allowed maximum is rejected at creation. Keep your spread reasonable.
</Warning>

## Example

A `fixed` markup of `4` on a 1000 GMD off-ramp: the base might be ~13.62 USDT,
and your user pays ~17.62 USDT (base + 4). You earn the 4 (settled as dalasi)
when the payment completes.

<CodeGroup>

```bash cURL
curl -X POST https://api.modempay.com/v1/minting/offramp \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_API_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "gmd_amount": 1000,
    "destination": "mobile_money",
    "payout_method": {
      "network": "wave",
      "account_number": "7000000",
      "account_name": "Account Holder"
    },
    "markup_config": {
      "type": "fixed",
      "value": 4
    }
  }'
```

```typescript nodejs
const offramp = await modempay.offramp.create({
  gmd_amount: 1000,
  destination: "mobile_money",
  payout_method: {
    network: "wave",
    account_number: "7000000",
    account_name: "Account Holder",
  },
  markup_config: {
    type: "fixed",
    value: 4,
  },
});
```

</CodeGroup>

Compared to a regular off-ramp: **`pay_amount`** now includes your markup, and
**`markup_share_usd`** shows the markup you'll earn (in USD).

## How settlement works

- The **base** settles the `gmd_amount`, to your balance, or to the mobile
  money wallet in `payout_method`, exactly as in a regular off-ramp.
- Your **markup** is settled to your Modem Pay balance, as a separate credit
  from the base for clarity.
- Both settle proportionally as partial payments arrive, and complete together
  when the full amount is paid.

<Note>
  When settling the base directly to mobile money, your markup is still credited
  to your Modem Pay balance (not sent to the wallet). This keeps your earnings
  separate from your user's payout.
</Note>

## Notes

- `markup_share_usd` is fixed at creation and does not change if the rate moves
  later.
- The markup is your revenue; the standard Modem Pay fee still applies to the
  base.
- For payment, status values, and webhooks, see the regular
  [Off-Ramp](/documentation/defi/offramp) page.
