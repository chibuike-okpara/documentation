Payouts (called **transfers** in the API) send money from your [payout balance](/documentation/core/balances) to a mobile money wallet. Use them for withdrawals, paying suppliers, salaries, winnings and refunds.

## How a payout works

<Steps>
  <Step title="You send the request">
    With the amount, network, wallet number, the person's name and a unique `Idempotency-Key`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="We check it">
    Your balance must cover the amount plus the fee. We also run fraud and limit checks.
  </Step>
  <Step title="We send the money">
    The amount and fee leave your payout balance and the money goes to the wallet, usually within seconds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You get the result">
    A `transfer.succeeded` or `transfer.failed` webhook. If a payout fails, the money goes back to your balance.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Where you can send money

| Network | `network` value | Wallet number |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wave | `wave` | 7 or 9 digits |
| Afrimoney | `afrimoney` | 7 or 9 digits |

To get the current list for your account, call `GET /v1/transfers/payout-channels`.

## The transfer object

```json
{
  "id": "45eb796c-e66a-442f-bba2-d282053f8883",
  "type": "mobile-money",
  "status": "completed",
  "amount": 100,
  "fee": 1,
  "currency": "GMD",
  "network": "afrimoney",
  "account_number": "7834567",
  "account_name": "Awa Jallow",
  "transfer_reference": "5Np7iVHrN",
  "balance_before": 995,
  "balance_after": 894,
  "note": "April supplies",
  "metadata": { "invoice_id": "inv_204" },
  "test_mode": true,
  "createdAt": "2025-05-05T21:12:52.839Z"
}
```

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `pending` | We're sending it. |
| `pending_review` | Our fraud checks are holding it. Review it in the dashboard. |
| `completed` | The money reached the wallet. |
| `failed` | It didn't go through. The money is back in your balance. |
| `cancelled` | It was stopped before sending. The money is back in your balance. |
| `reversed` | The money came back after it was sent. It's back in your balance. |

## Webhooks

| Event | Sent when |
| --- | --- |
| `transfer.succeeded` | The money reached the wallet. |
| `transfer.failed` | The payout failed. Your balance has been refunded. |
| `transfer.reversed` | The money came back, either because you reversed it, or because nobody claimed a Wave payout. Your balance has been refunded. |
| `transfer.flagged` | Our fraud checks are holding the payout for review. |

## Protect your balance

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Use a restricted key with payout limits">
    Create a key with only the `transfers` permission, a per-payout limit and a daily limit, and lock it to your servers' IP addresses. Even if it leaks, the damage is capped. See [API keys](/documentation/authentication#payout-limits).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Always send an Idempotency-Key">
    If your request times out and you retry with the same key, we won't pay twice.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Watch for transfer.flagged">
    Flagged payouts need your review in the dashboard before more money can go to that number.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
